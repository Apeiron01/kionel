FREE TOOL

Reconcile expected and received sample IDs.

Free and open source: up to 10,000 IDs per list. Compare two lists without uploading their contents. One ID per line; paste an Excel column without its header. Leading zeroes and case are preserved.

This checks textual identifiers, not physical sample identity, analytical results or regulatory compliance. The JSON report is unsigned; its hashes describe the supplied text, not who supplied it. No list is stored between visits.
