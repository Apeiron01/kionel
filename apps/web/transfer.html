Flashing images

Sending shows codes that change up to 60 times a second. This can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.

  • If you or anyone who can see this screen has photosensitive epilepsy, do not start the transfer.
  • Look away from the sending screen while it runs; only the receiving camera needs to see it.
  • If you feel dizzy or unwell, stop at once and look away.

Put this licence on another device

Two ways to put the same licence on another device. You only do this once per device.

Way 1 · With the other device's camera

On this device (the one that already has the licence) press Show licence as a code. On the other device open Receive, choose the live camera and point it at the code.

Way 2 · Without a camera: copy and paste

Do this on the device that needs the licence. Open kionel-license.json with a text editor (Notepad on Windows, TextEdit on Mac, or any notes app), select all of it, copy, and paste it into the box below. It is plain text that starts with {"schema" — copy all of it, from the first { to the last }.

Loaded once per browser. Keep kionel-license.json on a shared drive: you load it again after the browser's data is cleared, in a private window, or on another browser or computer.
