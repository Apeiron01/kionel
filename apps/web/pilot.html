OPEN SOURCE · SERVICES

Free to use. We help you put it into service.

Kionel is free and open source: every feature, no licence file, no account. What a laboratory or plant usually needs on top is someone to fit it to a real instrument workflow, document it for an audit and keep it running. That is what we sell.

Workflow integrationWe map one instrument or station export (folder, CSV, Excel, PDF) to the right Kionel route: screen and camera, printed pages, USB with signed records, or a one-way serial cable. You get the site policy, receiver cards, a tested setup and a short operator guide.

How it works

  1. Write to us with the instrument or station, what leaves it, where it must arrive and which rules apply. No real data, please.
  2. A 30-minute call to agree the scope.
  3. A fixed-price quote for that scope, in writing. Work is remote by default.
  4. Handover: the setup, the documents and the test evidence are yours. Everything runs without us.

Who you work with

Kionel is built by Taha Bayar at Lumina Digitale (Ankara, Türkiye), who also wrote the method preprint. You deal with the person who wrote the code. That is also a continuity risk for an institution, which is why the source is open and the verifier is permanently Apache-2.0: if we disappear, your records stay verifiable and the software stays usable.

What stays true

  • File content never reaches our server; the transfer happens between your two devices.
  • Services never require you to send us your data.
  • Kionel has not had an independent security audit yet. The security page lists the current status and the limits page what it does not do.
