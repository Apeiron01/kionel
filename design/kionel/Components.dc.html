POINTER · MAGNETIC ACTION
Request a pilot

The primary action leans toward the cursor inside its own hit area, up to 10 px, and returns when the pointer leaves. It makes the target feel weighted — and it widens the effective hit area before the click.

POINTER · FIELD DEPTH

Two module layers track the pointer at different rates, 8 and 16 px. This is the only depth effect in the system and it appears once, in the hero. Parallax anywhere else reads as decoration.
