Security is never a paid feature. End-to-end encryption, the two-factor confirmation code, the dual-signed receipt and the audit chain are identical in every tier, including the free one. A weakened free tier would be a dangerous free tier.

Where we do not compete

—Throughput. A diode moves gigabits. We move tens of kilobytes per second. Wrong tool for bulk flows, backups or log shipping.
—Certification. Diode vendors hold Common Criteria style approvals. We hold none, and will not bid where one is mandatory.
—Round-the-clock operations. Enterprise buys defined response times, not a staffed security operations centre.

Said at the first meeting rather than the last. It costs far less that way — for both sides.

What can be tailored

Negotiable: scope, response times, running the enrolment authority on your side, training, payment schedule, term, source escrow.

Not negotiable: the protocol, the cryptography and the published readiness state. No customer gets a cheaper, weaker build, and no open gate is shown as closed.
