What this does not protect against

—A compromised endpoint. If the operating system is owned, the data is already readable there.
—An operator who skips the code comparison. That step is what stops an interposed key.
—Traffic analysis. An observer sees that a transfer happened, and roughly how large.
—Identity. A receipt binds two keys, not two people.
